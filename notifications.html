Notification Setup
Alerts on every phone.

Two minutes per device and your team gets notified the instant a guest needs something — even with the phone in their pocket. Each staff member does this once on their own phone.

iPhone & iPad
Setting up on iPhone
1
Open Safari and go to your staff dashboard: favvi.ai/portal/your-hotel/staff. (It must be Safari — other browsers can't install apps on iPhone.)
2
Tap the Share button — the square with the arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen.
3
Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen", then tap Add. Favvi now appears on the home screen like a real app.
4
Open Favvi from the new home screen icon (not from Safari) and log in with your name and the staff password.
5
Tap "Enable Alerts" at the top of the dashboard, then tap Allow when iPhone asks. Done — requests will now pop up like text messages.
Android
Setting up on Android
1
Open Chrome and go to your staff dashboard: favvi.ai/portal/your-hotel/staff.
2
Tap the ⋮ menu in the top-right corner and choose "Add to Home screen" (sometimes called "Install app"), then confirm.
3
Open Favvi from the home screen icon and log in with your name and the staff password.
4
Tap "Enable Alerts", then Allow. That's it — alerts arrive even when the phone is locked.
Good to know

iPhone needs iOS 16.4 or newer for notifications — that's any iPhone updated since March 2023. If "Enable Alerts" says not supported, update iOS first.

Pick your department when logging in — housekeeping staff only get housekeeping alerts, maintenance only maintenance, and so on. Choose "All Departments" to receive everything.

The in-app chime respects the silent switch — if a phone is on silent, the dashboard won't make a sound, but push notifications still come through with their own alert.
