How It Works

A front desk that runs itself.

Three steps. No integrations, no training, no IT department needed.

Step one
Guests scan a QR code

Print-ready QR codes for guest rooms, reception, and key cards — generated for you. Guests scan and chat instantly in their browser. Nothing to download, nothing to install.

Step two
Favvi resolves it

Wifi passwords, breakfast hours, late checkout, local tips — answered instantly from your hotel's own knowledge, in the guest's language, at any hour of the night.

Towels, maintenance, room service — real requests are routed to the right department with a push notification. Your team sees everything on a live dashboard and claims tasks in one tap.

Questions

Asked & answered.

How long does setup actually take?

About 5 minutes. You sign up, paste your hotel information — rooms, facilities, breakfast hours, wifi details — into Settings, and print your QR codes. That's it. No integrations, no API keys, no IT team.

Does it connect to my booking system?

It doesn't need to. Favvi works alongside whatever you already use — it handles guest communication and staff requests, not reservations. That's why it takes minutes to set up instead of months.

What happens when the AI can't help?

It alerts your team. Any request that needs a human — towels, maintenance, room service — is routed to the right department with a push notification and appears on the live staff dashboard. The AI handles the routine; your team handles the rest.

Do guests need to download anything?

No. Guests scan a QR code and the chat opens in their browser instantly. It works on any phone, in any language, with nothing to install.

What if I want to cancel?

Cancel any time — there are no contracts. If you cancel during your 14-day trial, you pay nothing at all.
