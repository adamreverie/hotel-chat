What Favvi Does

One platform.
Every touchpoint.

From the moment a guest checks in to the review they leave after checkout — Favvi handles the entire guest experience loop.

01
AI Guest Concierge

A personal concierge in every guest's pocket — accessible via QR code in their room or your hotel website. Answers questions, handles requests, and notifies the right department instantly. Replies in the guest's own language with no setup required.

Guest Facing
02
Live Staff Dashboard

Every guest request appears in real time, colour coded by department — housekeeping, room service, maintenance, concierge. Staff claim and complete tasks from any device. Sound alerts for new requests. Nothing gets missed, nothing falls through the cracks.

Staff Facing
03
Staff AI Assistant

An internal AI trained on your hotel's own procedures and policies. Staff ask it how to handle complaints, process upgrades, or navigate any situation — and get instant, hotel-specific guidance. Makes onboarding new staff effortless and keeps your team consistent.

Staff Facing
04
Feedback and Analytics

Send personalised feedback links to guests at checkout. Emoji-rated scores across overall experience, cleanliness, staff, dining and WiFi flow directly into your analytics dashboard. Spot trends, identify issues early, and track improvement over time.
